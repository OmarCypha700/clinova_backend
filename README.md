# ClinOva

**Nursing practical assessments, done digitally.**

ClinOva replaces paper score sheets for the practical and clinical
examinations at nursing and midwifery training colleges. Examiners score
students step by step on a phone or tablet. Two examiners assess every
procedure independently, and their scores are reconciled into one fair final
mark. Administrators see every assessment, grade and care plan in one place.

> This repository is the **ClinOva server**: it stores the data and enforces
> the assessment rules behind the ClinOva web app.

---

## Why ClinOva

- **Fairer marks.** Two examiners score every procedure without seeing each
  other's scores, and differences are settled by a clear, rule-based
  reconciliation instead of a negotiation.
- **No paperwork.** No score sheets to print, collect, add up or lose. Scores
  are saved the moment they are entered.
- **Results straight away.** Totals, percentages and grades are worked out
  automatically and can be exported for the exam board.
- **Your institution's look.** Each college gets its own name, logo and brand
  colour, without any custom development.

## Key features

### Step-by-step scoring
- Every procedure is broken into its individual steps, each scored from
  **0 to 4** against the college's own rubric.
- Large, tap-friendly score buttons designed for use on the ward or in the
  skills lab, on a phone or a tablet.
- **Every score saves as it is entered**, so a dropped connection or a closed
  browser never loses work.
- A live progress bar shows how many steps are done, and a single status line
  says exactly where the assessment stands.

### Two-examiner assessment with reconciliation
- Each student's procedure is assessed by **two independent examiners**.
- **Blind scoring:** examiners cannot see each other's marks while they are
  assessing.
- Once both have finished, the reconciling examiner sees both sets of scores
  side by side, with disagreements highlighted.
- **Rule-based reconciliation:** the final score for each step can only fall
  between the two examiners' scores, keeping the outcome fair and
  defensible.
- The examiner who finishes scoring last becomes the reconciler, so there is
  never confusion about who settles the final mark. Every reconciliation
  records who did it and when.

### Care plans
- Score students' nursing care plans in a single-examiner assessment.
- Optionally allow a **second care plan** per student.
- Choose which programmes and levels require care plans.
- Lock care plans after submission, or allow rescoring when needed.

### Grades and results
- Totals, percentages and grades (**Distinction, Credit, Pass, Fail**) are
  calculated automatically.
- A grade distribution at a glance, with filters by programme, level and
  grade.
- Export results to **CSV, Excel or PDF**.

### Administration
- **Dashboard** with a live overview of assessments in progress, scored and
  reconciled.
- **Student procedures** view: every assessment for every student, with both
  examiners, scores and status, plus powerful filters and export.
- Manage **programmes, levels, students, examiners, procedures and
  categories** in one place.
- **Bulk import** students, examiners, procedures and procedure steps from
  Excel or CSV, using ready-made templates.
- Bulk actions: activate, deactivate or remove many records at once.

### Your institution, your brand
- Set your **institution's name, short name and logo**. They appear on the
  sign-in page, browser tabs and the installed app.
- Pick a **brand colour** from curated swatches or your own. ClinOva applies it
  across buttons, highlights and badges, and warns you if a colour would be
  hard to read.
- **Feature settings** let each college switch behaviour on or off to match
  its own exam rules.

### Built for real-world use
- **Works on any device:** phones, tablets, laptops and desktops.
- **Installable** on a phone's home screen like a native app.
- **Accessible:** full keyboard support, screen-reader labels, large touch
  targets and the highly legible Atkinson Hyperlegible typeface.
- **Clear, consistent status colours** throughout: pending, ready, scored and
  reconciled always look the same.

### Secure by design
- Separate **administrator and examiner roles**, each seeing only what they
  need.
- Secure sign-in sessions, strong-password rules and rate limiting that slows
  down repeated sign-in attempts.
- Examiners never see a co-examiner's scores until reconciliation, and locked
  results cannot be changed.

---

## Who it's for

**Administrators and exam officers** set up programmes, students, examiners
and procedures, follow assessments as they happen, and publish results.

**Examiners** find a student in seconds, score procedures and care plans
quickly on any device, and reconcile with their co-examiner.

**Institutions** get consistent, auditable practical exam results, with
less admin and no paper.
